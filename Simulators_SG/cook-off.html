What to notice
  • With the defaults: 1 worker takes 60 s, 4 helpers 25 s, 12 helpers 15 s. These are your paper answers to Activity 2.1 (the 5 s per problem is only an example).
  • 12 helpers are 4 times faster, not 12 times: the fixed hand-out time does not shrink.
  • More helpers than problems (try 60) do not help any more. Set the hand-out time to 0 and see what changes.

What to notice
  • For 10,000 independent additions the GPU wins by a large factor, even though each GPU core is slower (Predict first, Chapter 2).
  • Raise “additions that need the previous answer”: those cannot be shared, so one slow core does them one by one. The GPU's advantage shrinks, and can even turn into a loss.
  • Try a tiny job (10 additions): the GPU's set-up time makes it slower. This is why not every task goes to the GPU (Activity 2.4).
