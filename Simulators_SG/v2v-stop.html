What to notice
  • With the start settings (36 km/h = 10 m/s, crossing 25 m away, braking 5 m/s², reaction 0.1 s) Car B needs 11 m to stop, so it can wait at most 1.4 s. The table rows are your paper answers to Activity 12.5: 0.1 m, 1 m, 5 m and 20 m travelled, and a crash only at 2,000 ms.
  • Set the delay to 2,000 ms and tick the backup plan (300 ms): Car B no longer waits for the late message. It brakes at 300 ms and stops 11 m before the crossing, the same as when the message is lost.
  • Raise the speed to 60 km/h: the stopping distance grows with the square of the speed, so even a perfect network cannot save Car B (the curve drops to 0).
  • The price of the backup plan is not shown here: a car that brakes whenever a message is late will sometimes stop for nothing.
