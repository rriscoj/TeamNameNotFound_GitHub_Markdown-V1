What to notice
  • With the defaults you get your paper answers to Activity 4.3: 6,220,800 bytes per frame, 186,624,000 bytes per second, about 5.6 billion times more than the temperature sensor, and 1.9 MB per second after filtering.
  • Challenge: set 60 frames per second (twice the data), then 4K at 30 frames per second (four times the data).
  • Set the compression to 100: the camera still makes tens of millions of times more data than the temperature sensor. Real cameras do compress, but a camera is never a small data source.
