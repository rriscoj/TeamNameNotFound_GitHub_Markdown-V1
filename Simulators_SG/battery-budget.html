Battery budget: why sleeping wins

A chip that is asleep most of the time can make a small battery last for weeks. Change how long the chip is awake and how much power it uses while asleep, and watch the battery life.

1) The imaginary watch from Activity 3.1

Awake share:

average current = (share awake × awake current) + (share asleep × sleep current)
battery life in hours = battery size in mAh ÷ average current in mA
