What to notice
  • Early episodes bump into walls a lot. After a handful of episodes the best route scores 95: 5 moves × −1, then +100 (your paper answer to Activity 10.2).
  • Nobody told the AI the map. The arrows come only from rewards.
  • Here even ε = 0 can work, because every move costs points, so moves not yet tried look better than moves that were. Compare with the prize machines in Chapter 11, where this does not happen.
  • Open “Change the rewards”: set the wall penalty to 0 and the prize to 0. What does the AI learn to do? Is that the AI's fault?

What to notice
  • The number of states is a product. Each new part multiplies it. The chart is on a log scale: each gridline is 10 times bigger.
  • The toy car with 10 other cars needs about 32 petabytes if every state needs 1 byte. That does not fit in any normal computer, so real systems use clever shortcuts and powerful hardware (Chapters 1 and 2).
  • Change “repeat” for the other cars from 0 to 10 and watch the explosion.
