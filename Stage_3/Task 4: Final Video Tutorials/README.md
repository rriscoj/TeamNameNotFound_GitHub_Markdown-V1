### Inside the Package:
*   **Video Tutorials:** An introduction video plus one lesson video for each section of the module.
*   **Caption Files:** Subtitles for every video.
*   **Video Guide:** Learning goals, viewing order, timestamps, links to the Study Guide and Test Bank, and the full narration scripts.

## 🎞️ Video Series Overview
The series is narrated by two characters: **Nova** (calm, exact facts) and **Byte** (playful analogies and jokes). Each chapter is presented as a "case" for learners to solve.

| Section | Chapters | Focus |
| :--- | :-: | :--- |
| 🖥️ **I. Inside the Machine** | 1–4 | How a single device works and what slows it down |
| 🌐 **II. How Devices Talk** | 5–8 | Networks, the cloud, and the edge |
| 🤖 **III. How AI Learns** | 9–12 | Behaviourist AI: learning from rewards and trial and error |
| 🛠️ **IV. Putting It Together** | 13–16 | Real-world limits, safety and ethics, and mini-projects |

### Every lesson video includes:
*   A real-world **mystery** to hook the viewer.
*   **One case per chapter**, explained step by step with animated diagrams.
*   **Demos** from our own [HTML simulators](../../Simulators_SG).
*   **Pause-and-predict checks** so learners can test themselves.
*   A **final challenge** and pointers to the matching Study Guide and Test Bank material.
