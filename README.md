## 📚 Selected Module & Project Purpose
**Module:** Hardware, Networks & Behaviorist AI

**˗ˏˋ Purpose and Intended Learners ˎˊ˗:**
This project is designed to teach beginners taking *Foundations of CS & AI* who have little to no prior experience with hardware and behavioral AI. Our goal is to build a comprehensive shared Markdown content base, which is then adapted into a Study Guide and Test Bank, PPT Slides, and Video Tutorials. By the end of our materials, learners will be able to define key terms, understand core hardware components, map out data processing workflows, and identify real-world AI applications and limitations.

## 🗺️ Module at a Glance
Our 16-chapter curriculum is organized into four sections. Every chapter is presented as a detective "case" for learners to solve, guided by our two narrators, **Nova** and **Byte**.

| Section | Chapters | Focus |
| :--- | :-: | :--- |
| 🖥️ **I. Hardware Foundations: Inside the Machine** | 1–4 | How a single device works and what slows it down |
| 🌐 **II. Network Infrastructure: How Devices Talk** | 5–8 | Networks, the cloud, and the edge |
| 🤖 **III. Behaviourist AI and Trial-and-Error: How AI Learns** | 9–12 | Learning from rewards and trial and error |
| 🛠️ **IV. Co-Design and Simple Projects: Putting it Together** | 13–16 | Real-world limits, safety and ethics, and mini-projects |

👉 For chapter summaries, learning outcomes, and prerequisites, see the [Module Chapters README](./Stage_3/Task%201:%20Final%20Markdown%20Content/a%29%20Module%20Chapters/README.md).
