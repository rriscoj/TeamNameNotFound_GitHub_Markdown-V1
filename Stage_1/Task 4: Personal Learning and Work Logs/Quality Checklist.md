### Quality Checklist

Before submitting, each student should check that:

- [x] My name, student ID, team, and reporting period are clearly identified.
  - **Comment: name, student ID, team name, and reporting period are clearly stated.** 

- [x] My dated entries were maintained throughout Stage 1 and describe specific activities.
  - **Comment: date entries are included and the description is already specified** 

- [x] My log covers both project contributions and course learning beyond my team’s module.
  - **Comment: each member log covers both the project and what they have learned in class** 

- [x] I distinguish my own work from the work of teammates and AI tools.
  - **Comment: they clearly explained which work was their, teams' work and AI-assisted work** 

- [x] Readable, captioned evidence sits beside or below the relevant entry, with no separate evidence section.
  - **Comment: they provide readable evidence and captions next to the relevant entries** 

- [x] My reflections explain specific learning, difficulties, decisions, or changes in understanding.
  - **Comment: they reflected on what they learned, the challenges they faced and the decisions they made** 

- [x] All significant AI use includes relevant interactions, verification, judgment, and learning.
  - **Comment: they explained their AI use, how they checked the results, and what they learned from it** 

- [x] The teacher can assess my personal claims without following external links or searching another document.
  - **Comment: their log includes enough details for the teacher to understand and assess their work** 

- [x] My account is authentic, and I can explain the work and learning described.
  - **Comment: their account is honest, and they can explain the work and learning in their log** 

- [x] My complete log is included in the team’s single, clearly organized Word or PDF submission.
  - **Comment: their complete personal log is included in the team’s Word or PDF submission** 

Before submitting, the team should also check that:

- [x] Every member’s Stage 1 personal log is included in one Word or PDF document.
  - **Comment: Every team member’s Stage 1 personal log is included in one document** 

- [x] A contents list identifies all members, and each person's log starts on a new page with their name, student ID, team, and reporting period.
  - **Comment: the contents list includes everyone, and each log starts on a new page with the required details** 

- [x] All logs cover the beginning of course learning and project work through October 24, with entries in chronological order.
  - **Comment: all logs cover the course learning and project work up to October 24 in chronological order** 

- [x] Formatting is consistent and readable while preserving each member's own writing and perspective.
  - **Comment: the document is easy to read and keeps everyone’s own writing style** 

- [x] Evidence, including significant AI interactions and verification, appears beside or below the relevant entries.
  - **Comment: evidence and important AI interactions are included near the relevant entries** 

- [x] Shared activities are described consistently, with each member's individual contribution clearly distinguished.
  - **Comment: shared activities are described consistently, with everyone’s individual contributions made clear** 

- [x] Each member has checked their own section, and any missing content or conflicting contribution claims have been addressed.
  - **Comment: each member checked their own log, and they fixed any missing details or conflicting information** 

- [x] The complete document opens correctly, requires no external links for assessment, and is submitted once as **TeamName_Stage1_PersonalLogs**, without compression.
  - **Comment: the final document opens correctly, needs no external links, and is submitted once as TeamName_Stage1_PersonalLogs without compression**
