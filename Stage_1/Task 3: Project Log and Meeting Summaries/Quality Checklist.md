### Quality Checklist

Before submitting, check that:

- [x] The document identifies the team and covers its work from the beginning of the project through the Stage 1 deadline.
  - **Comment: the document shows the team's work from the start of the project until the stage 1 deadline** 

- [x] Dated entries explain specific tasks, responsible members, and actual outcomes.
  - **Comment: each entries explain the date, tasks completed, members responsible, and results** 

- [x] Entries include useful learning, unresolved issues, and actionable next steps where relevant.
  - **Comment: each entry explain what we already learned, what are problems that still remain, and what we plan to do next** 

- [x] Meeting summaries identify attendees, key decisions, and follow-up responsibilities and deadlines.
  - **Comment: the meeting notes include who attented, what have been decided, and who needs to do the task and the deadline** 

- [x] Readable, labelled evidence supports important progress and contribution claims.
  - **Comment: the document includes clear and labelled evidence to show team's progress and each member's contributions** 

- [x] All significant project-related AI use is documented, including important interactions, verification, decisions, and outcomes.
  - **Comment: all important uses of AI are recorded including what we asked, how we checked the answers, and how we used the results** 

- [x] The contribution table includes every member’s name, student ID, supported score, and specific contributions.
  - **Comment: the contribution table includes every member's name, student ID, score, and specific work each member have completed** 

- [x] Dates, progress claims, and responsibilities are consistent with the team’s actual work and other submissions.
  - **Comment: all dates, progress updates, and assigned task match our actual work and other submitted documents** 

- [x] The teacher can understand and assess the records without following external links.
  - **Comment: the document includes enought information for the teacher to understand and it is without opening any external links** 

- [x] All members have reviewed the single Word or PDF document, and it is submitted without compression.
  - **Comment: team members have checked the documents, and we will submit it as one word or pdf file without compressing it**
