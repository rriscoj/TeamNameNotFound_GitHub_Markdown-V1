## Status definitions

- **Not started:** No work is evidenced yet.
- **In progress:** Someone is actively working on it.
- **Draft exists / awaiting review:** A file exists, but a reviewer has not confirmed it.
- **Needs verification:** Existing information conflicts or needs to be checked.
- **Completed:** Work is finished and the evidence has been checked.
- **Blocked:** Work cannot continue until a named question or dependency is resolved.

## How the five workstreams support one another

1. **Shared Markdown comes first.** It is the source of truth for definitions, examples, diagrams, activities, answers, and references.
2. **The Study Guide and Test Bank** turn the same content into independent reading and practice with explained answers.
3. **PPT Slides** convert selected explanations into visual sequences and a worked example.
4. **Video Tutorials** narrate or demonstrate selected concepts and processes from the reviewed source.
5. **The Live Mini Lecture** teaches one focused question from the same source and checks understanding with a short audience activity.

When the source Markdown changes, the owners must identify which guide sections, questions, slides, scripts, or lecture notes are affected and update them before final delivery. Internal dates above are planning targets; confirm the official course deadlines on LeXue.
