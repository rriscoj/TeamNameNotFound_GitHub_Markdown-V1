### Quality Checklist

Before submitting, check that:

- [x] The team information is complete, and every member understands their responsibilities.
  - **Comment: they have included the team information and made sure everyone understands their responsibilities** 

- [x] The learning goals describe what beginners should be able to explain or do.
  - **Comment: their learning goals clearly explain what beginners should be able to understand or do** 

- [x] The content outline provides meaningful coverage with a manageable level of detail.
  - **Comment: their content outline covers the important topics with not too much detail** 

- [x] The planned study guide, slides, and videos have clear purposes and share a common Markdown foundation.
  - **Comment: their study guide, slides, and videos each have a clear purpose and use the same markdown foundation** 

- [x] The proposed mini lecture focuses on one important idea and includes a concrete teaching approach.
  - **Comment: their mini lecture focuses on one main idea and explains how they will teach it** 

- [x] The schedule includes content review, production, testing, feedback, and revision before the relevant deadlines.
  - **Comment: the schedule includes everything and includes enough time** 

- [x] Tasks have identifiable outcomes, responsible members, internal deadlines, and current statuses.
  - **Comment: task have clear outcomes, assigned members, deadlines, and updated statuses** 

- [x] The workload is realistic and reasonably balanced, with important dependencies and risks considered.
  - **Comment: the workload is balanced for everything includes possible risks and dependencies** 

- [x] The document is easy to read, links work, and all members have reviewed the plan.
  - **Comment: the document is easy to read, the links work, and all members have reviewed the plan** 

- [x] Significant AI use is documented, and the team can explain and justify the submitted plan.
  - **Comment: they have documented their significant AI use and can explain the decisions made in their plan**
