## 1. Purpose

The team will create a coherent, beginner-friendly set of learning materials for the selected course module. We will first develop and review a shared Markdown source. The reviewed source can then be adapted into a Study Guide, PPT slides, video tutorials, a question bank and a focused mini-lecture. The goal is not merely to collect facts, but to explain concepts in a sequence that learners can follow and apply.

## 2. Intended learners and prerequisites

**Intended learners:** Beginners taking Foundations of CS & AI who may have limited prior knowledge of computer hardware, networks and AI behavior.

**Prerequisites:** Basic reading and reasoning skills. Define technical terms when first used. Introduce any necessary computing concepts before relying on them in an example. Do not assume learners already know networking protocols, AI model terminology or robotics terminology.

## 3. Learning outcomes

By the end of the learning materials, a beginner should be able to:

1. Define the important terms used in the module in their own words.
2. Explain the purpose and basic function of CPU, GPU, RAM and storage, and distinguish their roles in an AI system.
3. Explain how data moves across a basic network and distinguish latency, bandwidth and throughput.
4. Describe a simple AI or robotic behavior sequence using input, decision/behavior logic and output, using the definition of “Behaviorist AI” confirmed by the course.
5. Follow a worked example and explain the reason for each step.
6. Apply a concept to a new beginner-level question and justify the answer.
7. Identify at least one limitation, common misconception or practical trade-off.
