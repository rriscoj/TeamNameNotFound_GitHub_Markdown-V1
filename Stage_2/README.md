# Stage 2 File Folder | TeamNameNotFound
 
Welcome to the **Stage 2** directory for **TeamNameNotFound**. This folder contains all of our deliverables, documentation, and expanded learning content for the second stage of the *Foundations of CS & AI* project.
 
Our selected module is **Hardware, Networks & Behaviorist AI**.
 
Building on the Stage 1 foundations, this stage expands our Markdown content from the first developed chapter into the full **16-chapter curriculum (Markdown Content V2)**, which we then adapted into a Study Guide and Test Bank, PPT Slides, and Video Tutorials.
 
## 📂 Directory Orientation
 
To make grading and review as straightforward as possible, we have organized our submission into dedicated task folders. Each folder contains its own `README.md` with a short orientation, and either the relevant Markdown files or a link to the live deliverables in our team's Google Drive workspace.
 
### [Task 1: Markdown Content V2](./Task%201:%20Markdown%20Content%20V2)
Houses the complete, refined educational content (Version 2.0) that serves as the blueprint for every other Stage 2 deliverable.
*   **`a) Module Chapters/`**: The full 16-chapter curriculum, organized into four sections:
    *   `I. Hardware Foundations: Inside the Machine` (Chapters 1–4)
    *   `II. Network Infrastructure: How Devices Talk` (Chapters 5–8)
    *   `III. Behaviourist AI and Trial-and-Error: How AI Learns` (Chapters 9–12)
    *   `IV. Co-Design and Simple Projects: Putting it Together` (Chapters 13–16)
*   **`b) Feedback Based Review.md`**: How we addressed each point of the instructor's Stage 1 feedback in our V2.0 content.
*   **`c) Member Based Review of the Module Content.md`**: Our internal peer-review records, with members testing activities, checking test bank questions, and verifying accuracy and sources.
### [Task 2: Study Guide and Test Bank V1](./Task%202:%20Study%20Guide%20and%20Test%20Bank%20V1)
Contains the link to our interactive beginner Study Guide, the separate editable Test Bank, the checked Answer Key, and supporting activity files for all 16 chapters.
 
### [Task 3: PPT Slides V1](./Task%203:%20PPT%20Slides%20V1)
Contains the link to the slide deck for our learning module, designed to give a visual, beginner-friendly breakdown of the core concepts.
 
### [Task 4: Video Tutorials V1](./Task%204:%20Video%20Tutorials%20V1)

### [Task 5: Project Log and Meeting Summaries](./Task%205:%20Project%20Log%20and%20Meeting%20Summaries)

### [Task 6: Personal Learning and Work Log](./Task%206:%20Personal%20Learning%20and%20Work%20Log)
