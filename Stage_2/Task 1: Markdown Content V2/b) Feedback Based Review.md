# 📝 Feedback Based Review

**Purpose:** This document tracks the specific feedback our team received from the instructor during the Stage 1 grading and details exactly how we addressed and resolved each point in our Stage 2 deliverables, specifically our Markdown Content V2.0.

---

### 1. Refinement of Learning Outcomes
*   **Stage 1 Feedback received:** "The learning outcomes are too general. The task list should cover all the possible tasks."
*   **Action Taken in V2.0:** We completely rewrote the Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs) at the beginning of our Markdown content to ensure they are observable and measurable. Instead of broad goals like "understand networks," we updated the outcomes to require learners to specifically "Explain in simple terms how hardware components... and network connections affect how fast smart devices and AI applications can run" and "Explore through hands-on activities how real-world gadgets... make decisions". The master task list was also expanded to cover all drafting, source checking, peer testing, and cross-resource updates.

### 2. Completeness of the Markdown PDF Export
*   **Stage 1 Feedback received:** The teacher praised the work but noted that the Stage 1 PDF only captured the showcase chapter. The feedback explicitly stated that "the PDF should capture the main structure and content of the Markdown files, not only the showcase chapter".
*   **Action Taken in V2.0:** We expanded our Markdown content from the initial 3 chapters to the full 16-chapter curriculum. For our Stage 2 submission, we rendered the entire module outline (Sections I through IV) and every single core chapter into one comprehensive, readable PDF. This final export now fully includes all diagrams, real-world examples, beginner activities, answer keys, and references, ensuring the complete V2.0 content copy is inspectable in one file.

### 3. Log Formatting and Consolidation
*   **Stage 1 Feedback received:** 
    *   *Project Log:* "15/15, good job."
    *   *Personal Logs:* "15/15, excellent job but one team, one submission."
*   **Action Taken in V2.0:** 
    *   We maintained the high-quality chronological format for our Project Log, ensuring that evidence is placed directly beside each entry for the October 28 to November 7 sprint. 
    *   To address the submission format for the Personal Logs, we aggregated the work of all six members into a single combined Word/PDF document. The final file includes a contents list and a dedicated starting page for each member's individual reflections and AI-use records, strictly adhering to the "one team, one submission" rule.
